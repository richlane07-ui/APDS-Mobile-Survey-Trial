Quality and evidence rules

Blur, overexposure and underexposure are assessed on the live central region. A brief camera movement is tolerated. When poor quality persists, the page chirps and shows the reason. It does not certify that text is legible, and it never inserts guessed readings into the asset register. The zoomed camera frame and processed preview can be exported for comparison; use demonstration equipment or obtain permission before exporting customer images.
